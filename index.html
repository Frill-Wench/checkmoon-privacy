Checkmoon: Bedtime Checklist

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Checkmoon: Bedtime Checklist ("Checkmoon") is an Android app made by an independent developer in the United Kingdom ("I", "me"), the developer named on the app's Google Play listing. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. If you have any questions, email frillw.apps@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Your checklists, ticks and alarm times stay on your phone. There are no accounts, and I never receive or see them.
  • The free version of the app shows a banner ad from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's ad software collects some information about your device, as described below.
  • In the UK, the EU and Switzerland, the free version asks for your consent before ads can be personalised, and you can change your answer at any time in the app.
  • The Basic and Premium versions don't show ads, and the app doesn't request ads on them.
  • I don't sell your information, and the app has no analytics or tracking of its own.

Information that stays on your phone

To work, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • your checklists (profile names and tasks), which tasks you've ticked and the times you ticked them;
  • the alarm time you set, and your saved "Heading Out" checks;
  • your theme, plan and settings.

None of this is sent to me or to anyone else. It's deleted when you uninstall the app, or when you clear its data (Android Settings › Apps › Checkmoon › Storage & cache › Clear storage).

If you've turned on Android's own backup for your phone, Android may include the app's saved data in your Google account backup. That backup is run by Google under your Google account settings; I can't see it.

Your alarm

When you set an alarm, the app passes the time and the label "Checkmoon" to your phone's Clock app, which then keeps and rings the alarm. This happens on your phone; nothing is sent over the internet.

Ads in the free version

The free version shows a banner ad at the bottom of the screen, provided by Google's AdMob service. To choose, show and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent fraud, Google's ad software in the app may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (a resettable ID provided by Android);
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location;
  • information about your device and the app, such as the phone model, Android version, language and app version;
  • how you interact with ads, such as whether an ad was shown or tapped.

Google uses this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. I don't receive this information about you; AdMob gives me only overall totals, such as how many ads were shown.

Your choices

  • Consent (UK, EU and Switzerland). The first time the free version shows an ad, Google's consent message asks whether ads can use your information. You can agree, choose what to allow, or not consent. If you don't consent, you may still see ads, but they won't be personalised.
  • Change your answer. In the app, go to Settings › About › Ad privacy choices.
  • Your advertising ID. You can reset or delete it in your phone's settings, usually under Settings › Privacy › Ads, Settings › Security & privacy › More privacy settings › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads, depending on the phone.
  • No ads at all. The Basic and Premium versions don't show ads. Uninstalling the app stops all of the above.

What the app doesn't do

The app has no sign-in or accounts, no analytics, and doesn't read your contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or precise location. It uses the internet only to load ads in the free version.

Children

Checkmoon isn't aimed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has used the free version and want to ask about it, please get in touch.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you have rights over your personal information, including to access it, correct it, delete it and object to how it's used. Because the app keeps your checklists on your phone and I don't receive your data, I usually won't hold anything about you. You can delete the app's data at any time as described above. For information Google collects through ads, you can use your choices above and Google's own privacy tools. If you're unhappy with how your information is handled, you can contact me, or complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk (or your local data protection authority if you're in the EU).

Changes to this policy

If the app starts using information differently, I'll update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the app.

Contact

Email frillw.apps@gmail.com with any questions about this policy or your information.
